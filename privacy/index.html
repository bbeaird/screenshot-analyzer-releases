Screenshot Analyzer Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-23 · Developer: Brantley Beaird

What stays on your device

The Mac and iPhone apps read screenshots from Photos and never change them. Text recognition, sorting, your library and settings stay on your device. On a Mac, an OpenAI key you add stays in your Keychain, and optional encrypted backups go to a folder you choose.

Usage analytics

On by default. A new install sends nothing until the setup screen has been shown. It then sends these events to Segment, which passes them to Google Analytics:

  • app_opened, when the app opens
  • screen_view, with screen_name: on a Mac insights, find, job_search, settings or cleanup; on iPhone today, library, jobs, insights or settings
  • report_exported or report_export_failed, after Export → Report on a Mac
  • index_requested, when indexing starts on iPhone

Each event also has app (screenshot-analyzer), platform (macos or ios), app_version, environment (production), session_id (when the app opened), engagement_time_msec (always 1), a timestamp, a random message ID, and a random identifier that is new each launch and never saved. No screenshots, recognized text, file names or library data are sent.

Segment sees your IP address; the app sets the event's IP field to 0.0.0.0.

Turn it off with the switch on the setup screen or Share anonymous usage stats in Settings (General on a Mac, Privacy on iPhone). This stops future sends; events already sent are not deleted. See the Segment (Twilio) privacy notice and Google's privacy policy.

Other connections

  • Updates (Mac): Check for Updates, or automatic checks (off by default, Settings → Updates), fetch the update feed and any update you install from GitHub, which sees your IP address and request headers. No system profile is sent.
  • iCloud Photos: with Download originals from iCloud on (the default), Photos downloads any screenshot the app needs that is not on your device. On iPhone, previews can download even when it is off.
  • Cloud analysis (OpenAI, Mac): off by default (Settings → Advanced). Only screenshots you confirm go to OpenAI, with your own API key: each a JPEG up to 1,600 pixels plus up to 12,000 characters of its recognized text, with store: false.
  • Links: these open in your browser only when you choose them; search and calendar links include that screenshot's text.
